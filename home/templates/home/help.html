Help Center

Everything you need to know about Club Squirrel!

Welcome to Club Squirrel

Club Squirrel is a social game where you can meet new people, chat, and hang out with friends.

Getting Started

  1. Click Join on the main menu.
  2. Create an account if you are a new player.
  3. Log in using your username and password.
  4. Return to the main menu and select Play.

How to Play

Click Play on the main menu to enter the game room. Connect with other players and enjoy spending time with your friends.

Chatting with Players

Use the chat box in the game room to communicate with other players. Type your message and use the send button.

Remember to respect other players and avoid sharing personal information.

Player Settings

Click Settings on the main menu to customize your game experience.

  • Change your player name.
  • Adjust volume preferences.
  • Mute game audio.
  • Save your settings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't I log in?

Make sure your username and password are correct. If you do not have an account, register first.

Why isn't the game loading?

Check your internet connection and refresh the page. Try returning to the main menu and selecting Play again.

Why can't I send messages?

Check your connection to the game room. Try refreshing the page if you are disconnected.

How do I change my settings?

Return to the main menu, select Settings, adjust your preferences, and click Save.

How do I log out?

Return to the main menu and click Logout when you are signed in.
